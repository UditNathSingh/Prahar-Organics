Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs →

How can I place a bulk order?

+

You can place bulk orders by filling out the form above and selecting “Bulk Order / B2B” as your subject, or by directly emailing us at business@praharorganics.com. Our enterprise team handles custom quantities, packaging, and volume discounts.

Can I visit your farm or office?

+

Yes, we warmly welcome visitors, research partners, and retail buyers to our Basukinath farm facilities and registered office. Please schedule your visit at least 3 business days in advance using the contact form or by calling us so our team can host you properly.

Do you supply products for export?

+

Absolutely. Prahar Organics is fully equipped to serve international markets with APEDA-aligned compliance, phytosanitary certifications, customized moisture-proof packaging, and flexible Incoterms (FOB, CIF). Select “Export Enquiry” above to connect with our export division.

How long does it take to get a response?

+

Our direct customer support team responds to all online messages, WhatsApp chats, and emails within 24 business hours. For urgent commercial requirements, please reach us directly on our dedicated phone line +91 92295 82295.
